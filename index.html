Paper Snake

Own 100% of the map.

Leave your land to draw a tail, then loop back home to claim everything inside the loop. Your dotted trail is harmless, it only marks the loop you are claiming. Crash your head into another snake's body and you die, so make them hit yours. Eat orbs to grow longer and thicker. Walls just stop you.

Move the mouse (or drag a finger) to steer, or use A / D / arrow keys. Hold Space or click to boost.
